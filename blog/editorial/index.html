Editorial policy

Start with the sources

Official documentation and announcements are the primary sources for product changes, release schedules and technical claims. Company-reported performance figures are distinguished from independently verified results. Unconfirmed details are not presented as facts.

AI and translation

AI and automation assist with research organization, drafting, translation, explanatory illustrations and publishing. GitHub articles are English editions of my Korean Naver posts. AI-generated illustrations are labeled and are not presented as actual product screenshots or photographs. I do not claim that every article receives human review before publication. Confirmed errors are corrected.

Advertising and independence

This site may use Google AdSense to support operating costs. Ads are supplied by Google and do not represent an endorsement by this blog. Sponsored content and affiliate links, when present, are disclosed separately.

Corrections and contact

Send the article URL, the passage in question and a verifiable source when requesting a correction. Substantive corrections will be identified in the article. Contact: swchoi1994@gmail.com.
