How to apply for childcare leave benefits: Check everything from the 180-day requirement to the 12-month deadline

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.11 · 4 min read

Childcare leave benefits are paid through employment insurance. Before applying, check the 180-day insured period, at least 30 days of leave for the same child, and the application deadline, based on official Employment24 and Life Law guidance.

Money received from employment insurance while taking childcare leave is called childcare leave benefits. Before applying, check three separate points: whether your employment insurance coverage period is sufficient, whether you took at least 30 days of leave for the same child, and whether you are within the application deadline. This article is based on official guidance from Employment24 and Life Law. Since the individual amount varies according to ordinary wages, the calculation examples use hypothetical figures.

1. Check the two eligibility requirements first

First, as of the date childcare leave begins, you must have at least 180 days in the insured unit period under employment insurance. This does not mean that 180 days have passed since you joined the company; it means adding up the periods during which you were enrolled in employment insurance. Periods covered as a self-employed person, artist, or labor provider are excluded from this calculation.

Second, you must take at least 30 days of childcare leave for the same child. The leave does not have to be continuous, and periods used within the most recent 12 months may be combined.

2. The amount varies by the number of leave months

The monthly payment for ordinary childcare leave is determined based on ordinary wages within the following ranges.

- Months 1–3: 100% of ordinary wages, with a monthly maximum of 2.5 million won

- Months 4–6: 100% of ordinary wages, with a monthly maximum of 2 million won

- From month 7 onward: 80% of ordinary wages, with a monthly maximum of 1.6 million won

The minimum for all periods is 700,000 won per month. Example (hypothetical): If monthly ordinary wages are 3 million won, the amount is 2.5 million won for months 1–3 and 2 million won for months 4–6. From month 7 onward, 80% of 3 million won is 2.4 million won, but the 1.6 million won maximum applies, resulting in 1.6 million won. The actual amount varies according to ordinary wages and the leave period.

If both parents take childcare leave, the maximum may differ under the 6+6 special provision. The structure allows up to 2.5 million won in the first month and up to 4.5 million won in the sixth month. For single-parent workers, the maximum for the first three months is 3 million won. Check the special-provision requirements on Employment24.

3. Decide on the application deadline and method

The application period begins one month after the date childcare leave starts and extends until 12 months after the date the leave ends. It is safest to mark the deadline on your calendar.

Applications can be submitted through the Employment24 website, mobile service, or an employment center. To apply online, your company must first submit the childcare leave confirmation, so ask the human resources representative in advance.

The two guides also differ on the application cycle. The Life Law page says benefits should be applied for monthly and that the amount for a given month must be applied for by the end of the following month. Employment24 says that applications may be submitted monthly or in a lump sum. Confirm the actual deadline with the employment center.

4. Checklist before applying

1) Calculate whether your insured unit period is at least 180 days.

2) Calculate whether your childcare leave period for the same child is at least 30 days.

3) Ask your company for the childcare leave confirmation and documents verifying your ordinary wages.

4) If your scheduled working hours during leave are at least 15 hours per week or your income is at least 1.5 million won per month, you must report this when applying. The stated criteria follow the Employment24 page.

5) Check the application deadline monthly or at the designated intervals.

5. Points to note

This article is general guidance intended to help explain the system. The Life Law page states that amendments to the Equal Employment Opportunity and Work-Family Balance Assistance Act will take effect on November 27, 2026, and that it plans to review and update the content. Check the latest requirements on Employment24 and with the employment center immediately before applying.
