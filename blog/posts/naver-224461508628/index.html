How to export ChatGPT conversation history: Download it within 24 hours of receiving the email

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.01 · 3 min read

To preserve or transfer ChatGPT conversation history, request a data export in Settings to receive a ZIP file, but only eligible account types can request one and the emailed download link expires after 24 hours.

In this article

  • 1. Check whether self-service export is available for your account
  • 2. Request an export in Settings (web)
  • 3. Download it within 24 hours after the email arrives
  • 4. Open the ZIP file and check for the conversations you need
  • 5. What to check when the email does not arrive

When you want to preserve your ChatGPT conversation history separately or move it to another account, the first feature to look for is data export. According to OpenAI’s official Help Center, requesting an export in Settings lets you receive a ZIP file containing your conversation history. However, only certain account types can make the request, and the download link received by email expires after 24 hours, so it is best to know the steps in advance. This article was organized after reading the official Help Center and is not based on trying the process on an actual account.

1. Check whether self-service export is available for your account

According to the official Help Center, the accounts eligible to make a request directly in Settings are Free, Go, Plus, and Pro individual accounts, as well as eligible ChatGPT Edu workspaces. Business, Enterprise, and ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces cannot use self-service export, so you must contact the workspace owner or administrator. You cannot make a request while logged out.

2. Request an export in Settings (web)

After logging in to chatgpt.com, open Settings from the profile menu. Under Data controls, click Export under Export data, then click Confirm export on the confirmation screen to submit the request. The mobile app has the same menu, but if the app tells you to go to the web, the official documentation instructs you to continue on the web using the same account.

3. Download it within 24 hours after the email arrives

You will be notified by email or SMS when the file is ready. Preparation can take up to 7 days. The download link in the notification expires 24 hours after it is received, and you must open it while logged in to the same account. If the link has expired, you must submit a new request in Settings or the Privacy Portal. If a request is already in progress, the stated procedure is to wait until that request is complete.

4. Open the ZIP file and check for the conversations you need

According to the official documentation, the ZIP file contains conversation history and account-related data. After downloading it, immediately check whether the conversations you need are actually included. Conversations that have already been deleted cannot be restored through an export. Because this file contains account information, it is safer to check it once more before sending it through a shared drive or messenger.

5. What to check when the email does not arrive

First, check whether the email is in your spam or promotions folder. Second, if the account uses only a phone number, check your SMS messages. Third, contact OpenAI Support if it still has not arrived after 7 days. In summary, the order is to check the account type, submit the request in Settings, download it within 24 hours after the email arrives, and check the conversations in the ZIP file.
