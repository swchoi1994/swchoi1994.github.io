National Employment Support Program application order: From job-seeker registration to applying for the Job Search Promotion Allowance

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.10 · 3 min read

The National Employment Support Program offers employment services and living support, and this guide explains its eligibility types and four-step application process, from job-seeker registration to applying for the Job Search Promotion Allowance.

If you have lost your job or are looking for work again, you need to know about the National Employment Support Program. It provides employment support services and living support payments together. Unlike unemployment benefits, which have a similar name, people with short or no employment insurance coverage can also apply if they meet the income and asset requirements. However, the application involves several steps that are easy to miss. This article organizes the process based on the official guidance pages of Employment24 and Government24.

1. First, distinguish Type 1 from Type 2

The National Employment Support Program is divided into Type 1 and Type 2. Type 1 is the route for receiving living support after having your income and assets assessed. According to the official guidance, the income requirement is household median income of 60% or less, and the asset limit is 400 million won or less. For the youth special provision, the asset limit is stated as 500 million won or less. Type 2 is the route focused on participating in employment support services rather than receiving living support payments. Employment24 will guide you according to the applicable conditions during the application process.

2. The four application steps

First, register as a job seeker on Employment24. The official guidance also says to complete job-seeker registration first, before applying. Second, apply for the National Employment Support Program on the online application page, work.go.kr/kua, and wait for the eligibility determination. Third, once eligibility is recognized, create an employment activity plan and carry out the employment support program. Fourth, after carrying out the program, apply for payment of the Job Search Promotion Allowance. There are two application methods: online and in person.

3. How much is the Job Search Promotion Allowance?

The representative allowance received by Type 1 participants is the Job Search Promotion Allowance. According to the official guidance, you can receive 600,000 won per month for up to six months. If you have dependents, an additional 100,000 won per person is added per month, with the additional amount capped at 400,000 won. To receive the allowance, you must carry out employment support and job-search activity support programs and apply for payment by the date that is six months from the date you receive notification of the eligibility determination. If you miss the deadline, you cannot apply, so it is a good idea to note the notification date.

4. Three points that are easy to confuse

First, the median-income and asset criteria change every year. Rather than taking the figures in this article as definitive, compare them with the Employment24 notice applicable at the time of application. Second, the Government24 guidance page for applying for payment of the Job Search Promotion Allowance has a record showing that it was last changed on November 7, 2022. That page does not state a processing period either. Third, many people register as job seekers but forget to submit the online application. The two steps are separate, so confirm that both have been completed.
