How to delete Gemini chat history and turn off saving: Check app activity settings

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.10.30 · 2 min read

If you are concerned about Gemini chats remaining in your Google Account, separate the history you want to delete from future history you do not want saved. The steps below are based on official Google help.

In this article

  • 1. Find the Gemini app activity screen
  • 2. Delete only the period you need
  • 3. Check the auto-delete period
  • 4. Turn off activity retention so future chats are not saved
  • 5. Check what remains after deletion

If you are concerned about Gemini chats remaining in your Google Account, it is best to configure separately the history you want to delete and the history you do not want saved in the future. The steps below are based on the Google Gemini app Help Center’s “Manage and delete activity” guidance. They reproduce the official help content rather than results I confirmed by changing settings in my own account.

1. Find the Gemini app activity screen

On a computer, sign in at gemini.google.com, then go to Settings & help and Activity. In the mobile app, tap Menu and your profile picture, then select Gemini Apps activity. Menu names may vary by device and app version, so if the names displayed on your screen differ, look for an item containing “Activity” or “Gemini Apps activity.”

2. Delete only the period you need

On the activity screen, tap Delete to choose from All time, Last hour or Last day, Custom range, a specific date, or specific activity. To delete just one conversation, select Delete item for that activity. Tapping Delete starts the deletion process in Google’s systems as well, so it is safest to copy any conversation you may need to find again later first.

3. Check the auto-delete period

According to the help page, Gemini app activity older than 18 months is automatically deleted by default. In Auto-delete settings on the same screen, you can change the retention period to 3 months or 36 months, or choose not to use auto-delete. This setting applies only when activity retention is turned on, so check it together with step 4 below.

4. Turn off activity retention so future chats are not saved

At the top of the activity screen, changing Activity retention to Turn off lets you configure new conversations not to be saved to your Google Account. However, the English help page says that even when activity retention is turned off, conversations may be kept in the account for up to 72 hours to provide the service and process feedback. It is best to check with the understanding that turning it off is not the same as disappearing immediately.

5. Check what remains after deletion

Chats created with a shared link may still leave the public link after you delete the conversation, so delete the link separately as well. Deleting a conversation shared with a specific Gem does not delete the Gem itself. For work or school accounts, a Google Workspace administrator may control retention settings, so if the menu is not visible or you cannot change the settings, check with the administrator.
