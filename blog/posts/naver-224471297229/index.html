The order for receiving unemployment benefits: Check the 180-day requirement through the 7-day waiting period

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.10 · 4 min read

After leaving a job, the first questions about unemployment benefits are whether you qualify and when and how much you will receive. This article organizes the job-seeking benefit criteria in order; amount examples are hypothetical.

After leaving a job, the first questions about unemployment benefits are "Am I eligible to receive them?" and "When will I receive them, and how much?" These two questions are assessed using different criteria. This article organizes, in order, the job-seeking benefit criteria presented in the Go-yong24 unemployment benefit system guide. The amount examples are hypothetical figures intended to show the calculation structure.

1. Eligibility: 180 insured-unit days in the past 18 months

Job-seeking benefits are considered when, at an employment insurance-covered workplace, the total insured-unit period during the 18 months before becoming unemployed (24 months for ultra-short-time workers) is at least 180 days. The insured-unit period includes not only days worked but also paid holidays for which wages were received from the employer and days for which business suspension allowances were received. Under a five-day workweek, public holidays that are not paid may be excluded, so check your employment contract or rules of employment first.

Other factors include whether you left the job involuntarily, whether you are actively seeking reemployment, and whether you fall under any grounds for restricting eligibility. If you resigned for personal reasons, it must be separately determined whether a justifiable reason for leaving is recognized. Go-yong24 states that a person dismissed for a serious fault is not considered eligible even if the separation was a recommended resignation.

2. Calculating the benefit period and prescribed benefit days

The benefit period is 12 months from the day after the separation date. Even if prescribed benefit days remain, benefits cannot be received after this period ends, so applying immediately after leaving a job is the basic approach. The prescribed benefit days are determined by age and the period of employment insurance coverage. For people who left their jobs on or after October 1, 2019, the period is 150 days for those under 50 with at least 1 year but less than 3 years of coverage, and 180 days for those aged 50 or older or those with disabilities.

Calculation example (hypothetical): Suppose the daily equivalent of the average wage before leaving the job is 100,000 won, the person is under 50, and the coverage period is 2 years. The daily payment is 100,000 won × 60% = 60,000 won, and multiplying this by 150 prescribed benefit days gives 9 million won. The actual amount varies depending on the method used to calculate the average wage and the upper and lower limits that change each year, so check with the Go-yong24 calculator or an employment center.

3. Application procedure and the 7-day waiting period

The basic procedure is to report unemployment without delay after leaving the job to the employment center with jurisdiction over your place of residence and register as a job seeker on Worknet. You must complete online training before applying for eligibility. Once eligibility is recognized, no benefits are paid for the first 7 days, which are the waiting period. The first unemployment recognition is approximately 2 weeks after the unemployment report date; afterward, you must receive unemployment recognition from an employment center every 1 to 4 weeks.

4. Checklist before applying

① Check whether the reason for leaving the job constitutes an involuntary separation. ② Add up the insured-unit period over the past 18 months using your employment contract and pay statements. ③ Report within 12 months from the day after the separation date. ④ Complete online training before applying for eligibility. ⑤ Mark the 7-day waiting period and unemployment recognition cycle on your calendar. ⑥ If you start work or receive income, report it according to the Go-yong24 guidance.

5. When the determination is unclear

If the reason for resignation is unclear or the insured-unit period is close to the threshold, it is better to consult an employment center before applying for eligibility. Eligibility is determined through an employment center review, and this article organizes the system's structure rather than providing a conclusion for an individual case. Since the upper and lower limits and period criteria may change, check the latest notices from Go-yong24.
