Order for switching to the Youth Future Savings Account: What Youth Leap Account subscribers should check from November 16 to 27

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.12 · 4 min read

Youth Leap Account subscribers should confirm eligibility to switch to the Youth Future Savings Account and follow the dates and steps carefully, using the handling institution’s app and official guidance for the final decision.

If you have subscribed to the Youth Leap Account, you should check whether you can switch to the Youth Future Savings Account. The order of the dates in this process is important. If you follow the steps incorrectly, you may be unable to make deposits into the new account. This article organizes the dates and procedures based on Financial Services Commission press releases. Because individual screening results differ, follow the handling institution’s app and official guidance for the final decision.

1. Check the dates first

According to the Financial Services Commission press release ↗, the second-round application period for the Youth Future Savings Account ended at 18:30 on October 16. Eligibility screening will take place from October 19 to November 13. Only people who pass the screening and receive notice that they are eligible to subscribe can open an account through the handling institution’s app from November 16 to 27. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded, and the account cannot be opened after this period. As of November 12, the period for accepting new applications had already ended. Therefore, this article is for people who have already applied or received their screening results.

2. Eligibility considers age, income, and household income together

The second-round applicants are young people born between November 17, 1991, and November 27, 2007. They must be aged 19 to 34 as of the account-opening date, while people who completed military service may deduct up to six years of military service from the age calculation. Their income must be annual salary of 75 million won or less (annual comprehensive income of 63 million won or less), or they must be a small business owner with annual sales of 300 million won or less. They must also meet the condition of household income at or below 200% of the median household income.

The benefits differ according to the income bracket. According to the explanation in the Financial Services Commission press release ↗, people whose annual salary is over 60 million won and no more than 75 million won receive only the interest-income tax exemption and are excluded from eligibility for government contributions. It is advisable to calculate your bracket in advance.

3. Switching order: Open the new account first, then close the existing account

1) Complete the Youth Future Savings Account application and screening, then receive notification that you are eligible to subscribe.

2) Between November 16 and 27, open the Youth Future Savings Account first through the handling institution’s app.

3) In the same app, apply for special early termination of the Youth Leap Account for the purpose of subscribing to the Youth Future Savings Account.

4) Once the termination application is received, the account will be automatically terminated, after which you can begin making deposits into the Youth Future Savings Account.

There are two points to note. First, if you do not complete the special early termination within the account-opening period, you cannot make deposits into the Youth Future Savings Account. Second, people who maintained their Youth Leap Account until its five-year maturity are not eligible to switch. The key is not to terminate the account as an end in itself, but to terminate it after receiving approval to subscribe to the new product.

4. Difference between the termination refund and ordinary early termination

The same press release explains that, in the case of special early termination, the termination refund includes government contributions and other amounts in addition to the money deposited so far, and the interest-income tax exemption is also maintained. By contrast, the Policy Briefing’s Youth Leap Account Q&A states that government contributions are not paid and the tax exemption does not apply in ordinary early termination. Because this Q&A is an early-launch document, check the estimated refund screen in the app and the official guidance again before terminating the account.

5. Checklist

① Confirm whether you have received notification of your screening result. If you have not received notification, you cannot proceed to the account-opening stage.

② Mark the weekdays between November 16 and 27, as well as the app’s closing time, on your calendar.

③ Open the Youth Future Savings Account first, then apply for special early termination of the Youth Leap Account.

④ After termination, check in the app whether deposits into the Youth Future Savings Account are available and how the Youth Leap Account termination refund is displayed.

⑤ Retroactive application of contributions under the 2027 budget proposal is a proposal currently under review by the National Assembly. Do not assume that the benefits will increase until an official announcement is finalized.
