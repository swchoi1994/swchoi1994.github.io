What will Siri AI on Apple TV 4K change in the living room?

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.10.16 · 3 min read

Hello, I’m Meotjin Aewol Nongbu.

Apple announced the new Apple TV 4K on October 13, 2026. The key feature of this announcement is Siri AI. It presents an experience where you can find content conversationally across the entire screen. Today, I’ll summarize what is changing based on the announcement materials and what conditions still need to be confirmed.

1 What has changed?

The example in the announcement materials is as follows. If you ask, “Which Apple TV shows won an Emmy this year?” poster images and follow-up recommendations such as “See more award-winning comedies” appear on the screen. You can also continue asking questions while watching, such as “Where was Pluribus filmed?” The answer is described as appearing in a small overlay that does not obscure the screen.

There are also requests that use personal context. If your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence and Siri AI is turned on, it can handle requests such as “Play the series my friend recommended.” The important point is that this feature assumes connectivity between devices.

2 Voice input depends on language settings

Apple introduced the built-in far-field microphones on Apple TV 4K as allowing you to speak without using your hands. However, a footnote in the same announcement materials says far-field microphone support is limited to cases where the default tvOS language is set to Chinese (Mandarin), Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Hebrew, Norwegian, or Swedish. Korean is not on this list.

Therefore, before applying the advantage of “convenient voice searching” directly to a Korean-language environment, you should first check the support scope. Based on the announcement materials alone, it is not possible to determine how or to what extent voice features will work in Korean.

3 Conditions and limitations to check

Based on the announcement materials and footnotes, the following conditions should be considered together.

First, the beta status. It is specified that Siri AI will be provided as a beta feature in tvOS 27.2.

Second, home settings. In the Home app, the owner of the home must use an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence and have Siri AI turned on. Siri AI access is also said to be available on the second-generation Apple TV 4K and later.

Third, regional restrictions. The announcement materials state that Siri AI will not be available from the outset on iOS and iPadOS in the EU.

Fourth, usage. Apple explained that Apple Intelligence features using server models have daily usage limits, and broader access may be offered for a fee in the future.

Fifth, launch scope. According to the announcement materials, preorders have begun in the United States and 29 other countries and regions. The release date and price in Korea were not confirmed in these materials.

4 Three things to check before buying

If it were me, I would check three things first. First, whether the home owner’s device meets the Apple Intelligence and Siri AI requirements. Second, whether the language used most often is included in the voice feature support list. Third, whether sales and pricing in Korea have been officially announced. If these three conditions are not met, the practical change you feel may be limited even if the on-screen features increase.

Feature introductions contain many descriptions such as “smarter,” but these announcement materials include no figures comparing response accuracy or speed. That aspect must be checked separately after real-world usage information becomes available.
