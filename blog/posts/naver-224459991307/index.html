How to reduce Windows notifications: Turn them off by app and schedule Do not disturb

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.10.31 · 2 min read

This guide explains how to reduce Windows 11 notifications by app and schedule Do not disturb using Microsoft’s official support settings paths as of October 31, 2026; it is not based on firsthand testing.

In this article

  • 1. Reduce notifications by app first
  • 2. Set Do not disturb and exceptions
  • 3. Check the conditions that turn it on automatically
  • 4. Three things to check after setup
  • Related article

When notifications keep appearing in Windows 11, they frequently interrupt your workflow. Before turning off all notifications at once, first identify which apps are sending them so you can keep essential notifications and reduce only the rest. The steps below were read and organized from the settings paths on Microsoft’s official support page as of October 31, 2026. They are not the result of my directly changing and testing these settings.

1. Reduce notifications by app first

Go to Start > Settings > System > Notifications. Turn off apps that do not need to send notifications in the notification list. If the list is long, rather than turning everything off at once, it is safer to start with the three apps that have been most bothersome recently. Keep essential apps such as Mail or messaging apps turned on.

2. Set Do not disturb and exceptions

On the same notification screen, turn on Do not disturb to reduce the notifications you see and hear. If there are apps whose notifications you still want to receive during Do not disturb, select them separately under Set priority notifications. Notifications received during Do not disturb are not deleted; they remain in Notification Center so you can check them later.

3. Check the conditions that turn it on automatically

Do not disturb can turn on automatically during set times. On an English-language screen, check the During these times option under Turn on do not disturb automatically. If unwanted times are set, turn them off and adjust the start and end times as well. You can also see the types of tasks that turn it on automatically in the same section.

4. Three things to check after setup

① Check for about a day to make sure important notifications are not being missed. If you turned off email notifications, also establish a habit of opening the email app directly to check it.

② If you do not want to block calls or alarms, allow calls and alarms under priority notifications.

③ If you only need a short period of focus, open Notification Center by clicking the battery, network, or sound icon on the taskbar, then temporarily turn on Do not disturb; this is simpler.
