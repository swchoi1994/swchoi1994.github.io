How to check dependent status for health insurance: Review support, income, and property requirements separately

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.10 · 3 min read

Family members of an employee-insured person may receive health insurance benefits without paying separate premiums if registered as dependents, but family status alone does not guarantee eligibility.

Family members of an employee-insured person may receive health insurance benefits without paying separate health insurance premiums if registered as dependents. However, being a family member alone does not automatically establish eligibility. This article explains how to check the support, income, and property requirements in order, as outlined in the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS) guide to acquiring dependent status. The calculation examples use assumed values.

1. Support requirement: Check family relationships and cohabitation first

Spouses, lineal ascendants (such as parents and grandparents), lineal descendants (such as children), and the spouses of lineal descendants are the basic eligible groups. Siblings are generally not eligible, and are recognized only when they meet exception requirements, such as being unmarried and at least 65 years old or under 30, having a disability, or being a national merit recipient, while also meeting the income and property requirements. The support requirements vary depending on family relationship and cohabitation, so refer to the service’s guidance table as well.

2. Income requirement: Combined annual income of no more than 20 million won

The combined annual amount of business, financial, pension, earned, and other income must be 20 million won or less. If the person has a business registration, there must be no business income; if the person does not have a business registration, business income must be 5 million won or less annually. Be sure to note that rental income from housing results in exclusion if there is any such income, regardless of whether the person has a business registration. For married applicants, both spouses must meet the income requirement, while a separate business-income threshold of 5 million won or less applies to some people with disabilities and national merit recipients.

3. Property requirement: Calculate based on the property-tax assessment base, not market value

The property requirement is based not on an apartment’s market value or sale price, but on the total property-tax assessment base for land, buildings, homes, ships, aircraft, and similar assets. There are three thresholds.

① Total assessment base of 540 million won or less: The property requirement is met.

② More than 540 million won and no more than 900 million won: Eligibility is recognized only when combined annual income is 10 million won or less.

③ More than 900 million won: The person is not recognized as a dependent regardless of income. Siblings are subject to a separate threshold of a total assessment base of 180 million won or less.

4. Review the decision process with hypothetical examples

Suppose that the parents of an employee-insured person’s child are applying to be registered as dependents, and assume that the support requirement is met.

A. Combined income of 15 million won and a total property-tax assessment base of 400 million won: Because income is no more than 20 million won and the property assessment base is no more than 540 million won, both the income and property requirements are met.

B. Combined income of 15 million won and a total property-tax assessment base of 600 million won: Because this falls in the range above 540 million won, income must be no more than 10 million won, so the applicant fails the requirement.

C. The person has a business registration and business income: The applicant fails the income requirement because business income must be absent.

5. Checklist before applying

① Prepare a family relationship certificate for submission that proves the family relationship. A certificate issued for viewing has no legal effect, so you may be asked to request a reissue. ② Gather annual income by category. ③ Check the total property-tax assessment base in the property-tax billing details. ④ If married, check the income requirements for both spouses. ⑤ The timing of income-data updates may vary, so check the service’s eligibility determination. ⑥ The service makes the final decision, so consult the service first if anything is unclear.
