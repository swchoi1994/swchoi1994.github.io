National Tomorrow Learning Card application steps: From job-seeker registration to course enrollment

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.12 · 6 min read

The National Tomorrow Learning Card supports vocational training costs. After issuance, up to 3 million won is available for five years, with an additional 2 million won for eligible applicants, while trainees pay part of the cost.

The National Tomorrow Learning Card is used to receive support for vocational training costs. According to the Ministry of Employment and Labor’s Employment24 program guide, after the card is issued, 3 million won can be used for training costs over five years, with an additional 2 million won for those who meet the requirements, for total support of up to 5 million won. However, trainees pay part of the training costs, so the actual amount payable must be checked for each course. This article lays out the process in order, from applying for issuance to completing the satisfaction survey after the course. Since amounts and criteria may change, also check the Employment24 guidance available when you apply.

1. Check excluded applicants first

Under the Employment24 guidance, anyone in Korea can receive the card, but some people are excluded. These include public officials, employees of private schools, and people aged 75 or older. Employees of large companies are excluded if their monthly wages are at least 3 million won and they are under 45. Special-type workers with monthly income of at least 5 million won are also excluded. Self-employed people with annual sales of at least 400 million won are likewise listed as subject to exclusion. If your eligibility is unclear, it is safer to check with the Ministry of Employment and Labor Customer Counseling Center (1350) before applying for issuance.

2. Determine whether job-seeker registration is required

If you are unemployed, you must first apply for a job on Employment24. The guidance explains that you must register on Employment24 as someone looking for a job before you can proceed with card issuance. Conversely, if you are already working, such as an employee, person on leave, self-employed person, or special-type worker, you generally do not need to apply as a job seeker. If you already registered as a job seeker while applying for unemployment benefits or the National Employment Support System, you do not need to register again. The application order for the two systems is summarized in the article on the order for receiving unemployment benefits ↗ and the article on the application order for the National Employment Support System ↗.

3. Apply for issuance and receive the physical card

You can apply for issuance online through Employment24 or by visiting the employment center with jurisdiction over your place of residence. Check the relevant employment center on My Page in Employment24. In most cases, no separate documents are required, but some applicants, such as self-employed people with a business registration certificate, may need to submit documents. Once the employment center decides to issue the card after its review, you can receive the physical card by mail or by visiting a bank. To receive it at a bank, bring the “National Tomorrow Learning Card Issuance Confirmation” issued by the employment center and your identification.

4. Choose a course and receive diagnostic counseling

You can search for and apply to courses directly on Employment24. If it is difficult to decide which education is suitable, you can receive diagnostic counseling to assess your job skills and get recommendations for necessary education and certifications. In particular, to take a long-term course lasting at least 140 hours, you must complete training diagnosis and counseling before applying for enrollment. Diagnostic counseling can be completed online through Employment24 or by visiting an employment center.

5. Calculate the self-payment and total charge

You pay the training cost with the National Tomorrow Learning Card when applying to enroll. According to the Employment24 guidance, trainees pay 0–55% of the training cost approved by the government. The rate is determined by factors including the average employment rate for the course occupation, whether the trainee receives the Earned Income Tax Credit, and the type of participation in the National Employment Support System. The self-payment is also charged to the card, while the remaining amount is deducted from the government-supported training cost. For example, if the government-approved training cost is 1 million won and the self-payment rate is assumed to be 20%, you would pay 200,000 won and 800,000 won in government support would be deducted from the card limit. This figure is an assumption for explanation; the actual rate must be checked on the course details page.

6. Check attendance and incentives during the course

If you do not have stable employment and receive at least 140 hours of training, you are also eligible for a training incentive. The incentive is deposited into the registered account without a separate application, and it is not paid if attendance during the unit period is below 80%. The amount varies according to daily training hours and the actual number of days attended. Under the Employment24 guidance, if training time is less than 5 hours, 2,500 won per day is paid; if it is at least 5 hours, 5,800 won per day is paid for the number of days attended. It may not be paid while you are receiving unemployment benefits.

7. Complete the satisfaction survey within 30 days after the course

After all education is completed, you must take the satisfaction survey on Employment24 within 30 days. If you do not do so within the specified period, the training incentive for the final month will not be paid. In addition, if you quit midway without an unavoidable reason such as illness, an accident, circumstances at the training institution, or a natural disaster, 200,000 won for the first occurrence, 500,000 won for the second, and 1 million won for the third will be deducted from your limit. If you may quit midway, check this criterion first.

Pre-application checklist

1) If you are unemployed, first apply as a job seeker on Employment24.

2) Apply for issuance through Employment24 or the relevant employment center, and choose whether to receive the physical card by mail or at a bank.

3) Find the desired course, and if it lasts at least 140 hours, receive diagnostic counseling first.

4) Check the self-payment on the enrollment screen, then pay with the card.

5) Maintain attendance of at least 80%, and complete the satisfaction survey within 30 days after the course ends.

6) The card is valid for five years. Once the period expires, all remaining balance disappears, so plan your training in advance.
