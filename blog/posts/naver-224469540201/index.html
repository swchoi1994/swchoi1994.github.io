Set up Windows Dynamic Lock: Automatically lock your PC when you leave with your phone

If you often forget to lock your Windows screen when leaving your desk, Dynamic Lock is worth considering. This feature automatically locks your PC when your phone is paired with it over Bluetooth and the phone's signal falls below a set threshold. This article summarizes the setup steps and precautions based on official Microsoft documentation.

1. First connect your phone to your PC via Bluetooth

Dynamic Lock operates based on the Bluetooth signal from a phone paired with the PC. Open the Bluetooth & devices menu in Settings and check whether your phone appears in the list. If it does not, pair it using Add device. Bluetooth must be enabled on both the phone and the PC, and the phone should be kept near the PC while completing the setup. Menu names may vary slightly depending on the Windows version.

2. Turn on Dynamic Lock in Sign-in options

In Settings, go to Accounts > Sign-in options, then find Dynamic Lock under Additional settings below. Microsoft Support instructs users to turn on the toggle for this option. The same document explains that when you leave with your phone, Windows automatically locks within about 1 minute after the phone moves out of Bluetooth range.

3. Understand the conditions that trigger locking

Microsoft Learn explains that Dynamic Lock locks the PC only when the Bluetooth signal drops and the system is idle. If someone is already using the PC, it may not lock even after the signal drops. Therefore, this feature is an additional barrier and cannot replace the habit of manually locking the PC with Win+L when you leave.

4. Steps to check after setup

1) Take your phone and walk a few steps away from the PC. 2) Wait about 1 minute and see whether the PC screen locks. 3) Return to your seat and sign in with your PIN or Windows Hello. This is a post-setup check procedure and is not the result of a test I conducted on an actual device. Whether it works may vary depending on the PC, phone, and Bluetooth driver.

5. When the option is missing or the PC does not lock

If the Dynamic Lock option does not appear, first check the Bluetooth pairing. You may need to remove the phone from the list and pair it again. If you are unable to sign in at all, see How to sign in again when you forget your Windows PIN: 3 reset methods ↗ first. If Bluetooth connects but there is no sound, What to check when Windows Bluetooth earphones connect but produce no sound ↗ may help.

6. Checklist

1) Paired the phone with the PC via Bluetooth. 2) Turned on Dynamic Lock in Sign-in options. 3) Maintained the habit of manually locking the PC with Win+L when leaving. 4) Do not rely on this feature on a public PC.
