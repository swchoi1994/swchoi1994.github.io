How to obtain a fixed date: Organizing online applications and priority repayment order

Seongwoo Choi · 2026.11.11 · 4 min read

A fixed date can determine whether a tenant receives a deposit first if a rental property enters auction or public sale. This article explains where and how to apply online, and the order relative to address registration.

After completing a jeonse or wolse lease, many people consider the fixed date a document they can obtain later. However, a fixed date is what can determine whether you receive your deposit first if the property enters an auction or public sale. This article organizes where to obtain a fixed date, how to apply online, and the order relative to address registration.

1. What a fixed date does

Article 3-2 of the Housing Lease Protection Act provides that when a leased home with a fixed date enters an auction or public sale, the tenant may receive the deposit from the proceeds of liquidation before junior security right holders or ordinary creditors. However, priority repayment does not arise from the fixed date alone. The opposing requirements, such as resident registration at the address and possession, must also be satisfied.

Opposability arises separately. Because opposability takes effect from midnight on the day after moving in, completing the address registration immediately after signing the contract is the basic approach. If you process the fixed date and address registration on the same day, you can arrange both effectiveness requirements at once.

2. Where to obtain it

You can obtain a fixed date at the eup or myeon office, dong community service center, or a branch office of the city, county, or district where the home is located; a district court or its branch; a registry office; or a notary public (Article 3-6 of the Housing Lease Protection Act). When visiting, bring the original contract and have the fixed-date stamp placed on it. Because operating hours and procedures differ by institution, it is safest to check before visiting.

3. How to obtain it online through the Internet Registry Office

Online fixed-date applications have been available through the Internet Registry Office (www.iros.go.kr) since September 14, 2015. The eligible document is a residential lease agreement, and commercial lease agreements are not eligible for online applications. You need an Internet Registry Office membership, a certificate for identity verification, and a scanned file of the original contract.

In the application, enter the property address, landlord and tenant information, deposit, and rent exactly as stated in the contract. Even a single different character may make acceptance difficult. A fee is charged; check the amount on the application screen. You must also check the submission hours. Applications submitted during weekday business hours are often processed the same day, but applications submitted late on a weekday, on Saturdays, or on public holidays may receive a fixed date on the next business day.

4. Criteria for determining the order

A fixed date is an official record that places a date on the contract. Therefore, it can be obtained on a date after the contract is concluded, and obtaining it on the contract date is the simplest approach. When multiple rights are tied to the same home, the fixed-date date and the date on which the opposing requirements were satisfied are used to determine priority. Therefore, keep the original contract and the fixed-date issuance copy together so that the dates cannot be changed arbitrarily.

5. What to check afterward

1) Keep the contract showing the fixed-date number and date of issuance, or the electronic fixed-date issuance copy.

2) Record the address registration date and fixed-date date together. This is because priority repayment takes effect only after both requirements have been satisfied.

3) When the contract period ends and you receive the deposit back, also check the certified copy of the real estate register and whether deposit guarantee insurance is available. The detailed sequence is organized in The order for reading a certified copy of the real estate register before signing a jeonse contract ↗.

4) If you are changing part of a jeonse deposit into monthly rent, refer to How to calculate when changing jeonse to monthly rent ↗ for the sequence for checking the conversion amount and upper limit.

Limitations of this article

This article explains procedures based on statutory provisions and guidance from public institutions. The Internet Registry Office screen layout, fees, and submission hours may change, so check the official site before applying. If an actual dispute occurs or a lawsuit to recover the deposit is necessary, seek assistance from a legal counseling organization.
